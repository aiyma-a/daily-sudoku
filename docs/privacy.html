Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · Русский · Кыргызча

Daily Sudoku is a puzzle game. It does not collect, send or sell any personal information. This page explains what that means.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

To let you continue where you stopped, the game saves these items in your device's own storage:

  • your puzzle progress, notes and Practice puzzle numbers;
  • your settings (theme, level, Daily or Practice mode, sound, vibration, language);
  • your statistics and daily streak (puzzles solved, times, days played);
  • a small "just right for you" profile, based on how fast you solve, used to pick puzzles for you.

This data never leaves your device. We cannot see it. The "Erase everything and start over" button deletes it. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app also deletes it.

Network use

The game downloads the daily puzzles and the app files from our website (GitHub Pages) so it can work offline. Like any website, the host may see technical details of a request, such as your IP address. We do not receive or use that information. We send no data about you or your play.

Third parties

We do not share data with anyone, because we do not have any. The game contains no third-party code for advertising, analytics or tracking.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if we add an optional feature that sends data, we will update this page and the date above before that feature is released.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/aiyma-a/daily-sudoku/issues.
